Optional vanity domain → Vercel 301

Only after QR + signs work. Goal: short door, not a second website.

  1. Pick name: getcutmygrass.com (alts book… / …wa) — confirm at cart
  2. Register on GoDaddy (you log in — not chat)
  3. Vercel project → Domains → Add vanity
  4. DNS: A apex + CNAME www as Vercel shows (edit the new domain only)
  5. Force 301/308 → https://www.blackrabbitlawn.com/cut-my-grass
  6. Verify in incognito before any new print with short URL
Never reverse-proxy dual-index two full sites. One canonical host: blackrabbitlawn.com. Don’t touch MX on blackrabbitlawn.com while adding vanity DNS.

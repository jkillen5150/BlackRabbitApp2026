By Jerry, owner

Roof moss in Thurston County grows all through the wet season, so the best time to deal with it is fall, before the heavy rain, or in spring once it dries out. We remove moss by hand: scraped and brushed off, with the debris cleared from the gutters.

Price: free quote. Cost depends on roof size, pitch and how thick the moss is. Gutter and roof moss work is done with a trusted partner while our ladder insurance is pending.

Why moss is a problem here

The South Sound stays damp and shady for months. Moss holds water against the roof, lifts at edges, and washes into gutters and downspouts, where it causes clogs.

How we remove it

Manual removal only. Moss is scraped and brushed off the roofing by hand, the pieces that land in the gutters are cleared out, and downspouts are checked for flow. Debris is bagged and hauled away.

When to book

Fall, before winter rain, or in spring when the roof is dry. Pair it with gutter cleaning, since moss ends up in the gutters anyway.

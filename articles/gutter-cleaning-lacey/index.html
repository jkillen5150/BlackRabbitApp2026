The best time to clean gutters in Lacey is late fall, after most of the leaves and fir needles are down, usually November. Homes under a lot of trees often need a second cleaning in spring.

Price: free quote. Cost depends on the length of the gutters, the roof height and how packed they are, so we quote every home after a photo or a quick look. Gutter and roof moss work is done with a trusted partner while our ladder insurance is pending.

Why timing matters in Lacey

Lacey gets most of its rain from fall through spring. Clogged gutters spill that water next to the foundation, onto steps and into beds, and wet debris gets heavy enough to pull gutters loose.

Cleaning too early in October means the gutters can fill again before winter. Waiting until most leaves are down gets you through the wet season with one visit.

Gutters cleared by hand, debris bagged and hauled away, downspouts checked for flow, and the walks and beds below cleaned up. Many Lacey customers pair it with a fall leaf cleanup or holiday lights.

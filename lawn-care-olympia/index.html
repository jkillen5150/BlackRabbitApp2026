Olympia is one of the leafiest places in the South Sound. On the older streets of the Eastside, South Capitol and Northeast neighborhoods, and up the Westside hills toward Cooper Point, big-leaf maples, Oregon white oaks and Douglas firs shade a lot of yards. That's a big part of Olympia's charm. It also means leaves, needles, moss and lawns that struggle in the shade.

What Olympia yards need most

Leaves, then more leaves. Mature maples and oaks drop over several weeks in fall. A wet mat left on the grass through winter thins the lawn and invites moss, and leaves on sidewalks and parking strips get slick once the rain starts. See fall leaf cleanup.

Gutters under the canopy. Fir needles and maple leaves fill gutters fast on tree-lined streets. We clear them by hand and pull moss off roofs the same way: gutter cleaning & roof moss removal.

Shade and a slightly higher cut. Grass under trees does better mowed a little taller, so we set the height for your yard instead of scalping it.

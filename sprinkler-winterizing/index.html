FAQs

How much does sprinkler winterizing cost?

$85-125 per house, plus about $9 for each extra zone.

When should I winterize my sprinklers?

Before the first hard freeze, which usually comes around Nov 1 in the South Sound. Earlier is safer.

What happens if I skip it?

Water left in the lines can freeze and crack pipes, fittings or valves, and you may not find the leak until you turn the system on in spring.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. We need access to the shutoff and the controller. Tell us where they are when you book.

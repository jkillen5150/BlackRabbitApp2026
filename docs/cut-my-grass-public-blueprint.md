## 1. Executive summary

**Cut My Grass (CMG)** is already a live, single-operator book-a-cut product of **Black Rabbit Landscaping**, not a separate company that needs a domain migration to “launch.” Public go-to-market means:

1. **Keep the product URL where it is** — `https://www.blackrabbitlawn.com/cut-my-grass` — so SEO, trust, LBI messaging, and ops stay on one host.
2. **Optionally register one vanity/CTA domain** (prefer `getcutmygrass.com` or `bookcutmygrass.com` / geo alias) and **301 it to that path** — do **not** dual-index a second live site.
3. **Do not chase `cutmygrass.com` first** — it is registered through 2029, parked/aftermarket-style, not free. Buy only if aftermarket price is justified.
4. **Win on three differentiators:** same named operator (Jerry) every visit; one clear local price with no marketplace fees; hyperlocal Yelm/South Sound reliability vs national apps and FB DMs.
5. **MVP is already shipped** for public use: multi-step intake → durable lead → optional **$25** Stripe deposit → track page → human text/call confirm. Multi-crew marketplace, SMS automation, paid ads, and trademark stay later.
6. **Distribution beats features for 30 days:** Facebook Reels/posts (already in progress), neighbor/Nextdoor wave, then **print + QR** pointing only at the canonical CMG URL. Goal window from project status: **~30 new site first-timers by ~2026-08-21**.

**Bottom line:** Go public as a **local product of Black Rabbit**, not as a new marketplace brand. Lowest risk = path-only (Option A) + optional vanity 301 (Option C).

Know somebody in need?

This isn't a coupon page. It's a neighbor-to-neighbor ask — if someone around here is in a real bind with their yard, we may be able to help.

Black Rabbit Landscaping believes in good stewardship of our treasure. One of the most valuable treasures we have is our time. If you know somebody who is in genuine need, we may be able to help.

Please submit their information here, or yours, and let's talk about it.

Shopping for a regular cut at our usual rates? That's great too — use the quote form or Cut My Grass.

What genuine need looks like

  • An elderly neighbor who can't safely mow anymore
  • A household going through hardship who would otherwise skip a cut
  • Someone disabled, recovering, or caring for a loved one who would go without yard care
  • A person who would otherwise sit with an overgrown yard because they simply cannot do it or pay for it right now

This is not a free-quote-for-anyone loophole, a discount code, or a way to skip the regular booking path. If it isn't genuine need, please book a normal cut — we're glad to help there too.

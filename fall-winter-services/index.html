Residential & commercial leaf cleanup in Yelm, Rainier, Roy, Tenino & Olympia

Around Thurston County, the big leaf drop usually runs from mid-October through November. A light scattering can be mowed right back into the lawn. A thick, soggy layer is another matter: it shades the grass, keeps it wet, and leaves you with bare patches and moss by spring. On paths and steps, wet leaves turn slippery as soon as the rain sets in, and the piles in gutters and along the foundation never really dry out.

What we keep in mind on every job:

  • Nothing goes in the street. Leaves blown into the road end up clogging storm drains, and cities around here expect property owners to keep them out. We bag or haul instead.
  • Your yard, your leaves. In Washington, leaves that fall on your property are yours to deal with, even when the tree is the neighbor's. We clean up what landed on your side.
  • It's not just maple leaves. Fir needles and cones add a lot of bulk here, so we plan for mixed debris.
  • Timing matters. One thorough visit after most trees are bare tends to beat several half-finished weekends. Bigger lots may need a second pass.
  • Want some leaves kept? Tell us. Shredded leaves can make good winter mulch in garden beds. A solid layer on the lawn usually does more harm than good.

We do this for homeowners and for businesses and HOAs: lawns, beds, driveways, walkways, parking strips, entrances and common areas. More detail: fall leaf cleanup · commercial & HOA upkeep.

Do you do commercial leaf cleanup?

When should I book leaf cleanup?

Most South Sound trees drop hard from mid-October into November. One thorough pass after most trees are bare usually works best, and some yards need a second visit. Book early; fall fills up.

Do you remove roof moss?

Yes, by hand. We scrape and brush moss off the roof and clear what falls into the gutters. See gutter cleaning & roof moss removal.

Do you winterize sprinkler systems?

Yes. We winterize irrigation systems before hard freezes so lines and heads are less likely to crack. Ask for it with your fall quote.

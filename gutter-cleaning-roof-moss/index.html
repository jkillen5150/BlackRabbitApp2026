When leaves and fir needles pack the gutters, rain runs over the edge instead of down the spout. In a South Sound winter that's a lot of water landing next to your foundation, on your steps and in your beds. Moss on the roof holds water too, and it keeps growing all winter.

Gutter & moss FAQs

Do you haul the debris away?

Yes. Gutter debris and moss from our work are bagged and hauled away as part of the quote.

How do you remove roof moss?

By hand. We scrape and brush moss off the roofing, clear the pieces that fall into the gutters, and check that downspouts are flowing.

Do you work on tall or steep roofs?

Tell us the height and pitch when you ask for a quote (a photo is best). If a roof isn't a safe job for us, we'll tell you straight.

When is the best time for gutter cleaning?

Late fall, after most leaves have dropped. Homes with a lot of trees overhead often need a spring cleaning too.

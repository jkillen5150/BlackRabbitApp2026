Common questions

When should I clean my gutters?

Late fall, after most leaves and needles are down, usually November. Homes with a lot of trees overhead often need a spring cleaning too. Read gutter cleaning in Lacey: timing and cost.

How much does gutter cleaning cost?

Every job gets a free quote, because cost depends on gutter length, roof height and how full the gutters are. Text a photo to (407) 951-1663.

When should roof moss be removed?

Fall, before the heavy rain, or in spring once the roof dries out. Read roof moss in Thurston County.

How do you remove roof moss?

By hand only. We scrape and brush moss off, clear the gutters and check downspouts.

Do you do gutter and moss work yourselves?

Gutter and roof moss work is done with a trusted partner while our ladder insurance is pending.

How much does holiday light installation cost?

Free quote. Price depends on the length of the roofline, the height and how many lights you want. See holiday lights.

When do you install and take down holiday lights?

We install, check that everything works, and set your takedown date on the calendar at install. We come back after the season to take them down. Install slots fill up the closer it gets to the holidays, so book early.

How much does sprinkler winterizing cost?

$85-125 per house, plus about $9 for each extra zone. See sprinkler winterizing.

When should I winterize my sprinklers?

Before the first hard freeze, which usually comes around Nov 1 in the South Sound.

How do you clear blackberries?

We cut the canes down, dig out the root crowns we can reach, and haul it all away. Every job gets a free quote. See blackberry clearing.

Do you haul away storm debris and brush?

Yes. Branches and debris are cleared and hauled away as part of storm cleanup, and cuttings go with us on clearing jobs. Every job gets a free quote.

What areas do you serve?

Olympia, Lacey, Tumwater, Yelm, Rainier and Tenino in Thurston County, plus Roy in Pierce County. See the service map.

Where are you based, and how long have you been in business?

Why is your phone number a 407 area code?

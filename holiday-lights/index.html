Nobody enjoys a wet ladder in December. Black Rabbit Landscaping installs holiday lights on homes and storefronts across the South Sound and comes back after the season to take them down, so you get the glow without the gutter-clip wrestling.

How it works

  1. Text a photo of the house or storefront to (407) 951-1663 and say what you want lit: roofline, trees, bushes, walkway, windows.
  2. Tell us whether you already have lights you'd like used. Jerry sends a free quote for the install and the takedown.
  3. We install, check that everything works, and put your takedown date on the calendar.

Holiday lights FAQs

Do you take the lights down too?

Yes. Installation and takedown. We set the takedown date when we install.

Whose lights do you use?

Yours, if you already have them. We hang and take down the lights you own. If you need strings, clips or replacements, say so on the quote and we will source them. We do not assume we are bringing a full light package.

Do you do businesses?

Yes: storefronts, offices and HOA entrances as well as homes.

When should I book?

In the fall. Install slots fill up as the holidays get closer.

How do I get a quote?

Text a photo of the house or storefront to (407) 951-1663 and tell us what you want lit, or use the form.

Which towns do you cover?

Olympia, Lacey, Tumwater, Yelm, Rainier, Tenino and Roy. See the service map.

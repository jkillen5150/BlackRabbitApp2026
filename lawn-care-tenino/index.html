Tenino is the sandstone town at the south end of Thurston County, where Old Highway 99 meets SR 507. People know it for the old quarry pool in Tenino City Park, the Tenino Depot Museum, Oregon Trail Days every summer, and Wolf Haven International just outside town. The Yelm-Tenino Trail ends here too.

Outside the few blocks of downtown, a lot of Tenino-area homes sit on acreage with pasture edges, tree lines and blackberry that never stops trying. Black Rabbit Landscaping helps keep all of that in check. Call or text Jerry: (407) 951-1663.

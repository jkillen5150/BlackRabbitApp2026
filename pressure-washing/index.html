Nine months of drizzle leaves driveways green, decks slippery and siding streaked. Black Rabbit Landscaping pressure washes driveways, walkways, decks, patios and siding for homes and businesses across the South Sound, so they look cared-for and are safer to walk on.

What we wash

  • Driveways and walkways: moss, algae and tire marks
  • Decks and patios: pressure matched to the surface, so wood isn't gouged
  • Siding: low-pressure wash for siding and trim
  • Commercial entrances and sidewalks: see commercial & HOA upkeep

Good to know

We work to keep wash water and debris out of storm drains, and we clean up the mess we knock loose. Pair washing with gutter cleaning or a yard cleanup to reset the whole property at once.

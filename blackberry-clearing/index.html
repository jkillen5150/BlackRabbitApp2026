Himalayan blackberry takes over fence lines, back corners and empty lots fast. Once it is thick, it hides trash, blocks paths and keeps spreading.

Black Rabbit Landscaping cuts back and clears blackberry and brush for homes and small businesses in Olympia, Lacey, Tumwater, Yelm, Rainier, Tenino and Roy. Every job gets a free quote.

What's included

  • Root crowns dug out where we can reach them
  • Cuttings loaded and hauled away
  • Area raked clean

Blackberry regrows from roots. A follow-up visit in spring catches new shoots before they take over again. Ask us about it with your quote.

FAQs

How much does blackberry clearing cost?

It depends on how big and how thick the patch is, so every job gets a free quote. A photo helps.

How do you remove blackberries?

We cut the canes down, dig out the root crowns we can reach, and haul everything away.

Will they grow back?

Blackberry spreads from roots, so some regrowth is common. A spring follow-up visit keeps it under control.

Do you haul the cuttings away?

Yes. Cuttings and debris from our work are loaded and hauled away.
